About

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at Cornell Tech, Cornell University, working with Prof. Mert R. Sabuncu. I obtained my Ph.D. from the Australian Institute for Machine Learning (AIML) at the University of Adelaide, advised by Prof. Gustavo Carneiro and Prof. Mark Jenkinson.

My research lies at the intersection of computer vision and medical image analysis. I develop weakly-supervised and label-efficient deep learning methods, with a current focus on medical radiology and cardiovascular imaging.
