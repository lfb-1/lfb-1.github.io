About

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at Cornell Tech, Cornell University, working with Prof. Mert R. Sabuncu. I received my Ph.D. from the Australian Institute for Machine Learning (AIML) at the University of Adelaide, supervised by Prof. Gustavo Carneiro and Prof. Mark Jenkinson.

My research focuses on computer vision and medical image analysis, with emphasis on developing weakly-supervised deep learning solutions including noisy label learning, semi-supervised learning, partial-label learning, and active learning, and their applications in medical imaging.
